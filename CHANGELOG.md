## 2026-10-22 · Pulse P3 (22 Oct)

- Block A (10 prompts on ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini) and block B (4 GCC prompts on
  ChatGPT and Perplexity), 3 runs, 114 measurements. Same conditions and counting rules as
  P1 and P2. Runs: 05:27–05:50, 10:39–11:22, 14:39–14:57 CEST.
- Grovia Digital named in 6 of 114 measurements, all on ChatGPT and all in block B: E16 in
  runs 1–3 (rank 2, 4, 3), E12 in runs 2 and 3 (rank 2, 2), E20 in run 3 (rank 4). These are
  the first ChatGPT mentions of Grovia Digital in the dataset. Block A: 0 of 90. Perplexity
  in block B: 0 of 12.
- Cited as a source in 9: the six ChatGPT answers above (source chip or link), D12 on
  Perplexity in runs 1 and 3 (answer text), E20 on Perplexity in run 2 (source list only).

  month. Grovia Digital changed its published price to AED 7,700 and AED 10,100 on
  3 Oct 2026, so the answers reflect an older state of a source.
- ChatGPT showed the usage-limit notice of a free account ("files, images and data analysis
  unavailable until …") from P3-D12-CHA-1 in run 1 and during all of run 3. Whether a
  different model answered cannot be determined from the interface. Run 2 ran without the
  notice and contains two of the six mentions.
- Sources file: Perplexity only (763 rows). ChatGPT and Gemini sources are not recorded
  per URL.
- One answer showed a paid placement below the answer (P3-E01-CHA-1); it is not counted as
  a provider mention.
- Spelling variants merged in P3: "High Street" as "High Street Agency", "Kreativ&Söhne" as
  "Kreativ&Soehne". P1 and P2 files are unchanged.

## 2026-10-08 · Pulses P1 (24 Sep) and P2 (8 Oct)

- Pulse = 3 runs of a fixed subset of the baseline prompts on ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini,
  VPN exit Frankfurt, memory off, new chat per measurement. P1: 10 prompts (block A) plus
  4 GCC prompts (block B), 114 measurements. P2: block A only, 90 measurements.
- Same counting rules as the baseline (one provider once per answer, spelling variants merged,
  individual persons replaced). Software tools are listed separately in tools_named.
- Pulses are compared only with runs 1–3 of the baseline, never with the five-run figures.
- P2: Grovia Digital named in 2 of 90 measurements (Perplexity, prompt D12, runs 1 and 3,
  rank 2) and cited as a source in 6 (D12 runs 1–3 in the answer text, D01 runs 1–3 in the
  source list only). ChatGPT and Gemini: 0 of 60. P1: 0 mentions, 0 citations.
- Times are CEST. Model versions were not shown in the interfaces and are "not recorded".

## 2026-09-10 · Baseline (first publication)

- 525 measurements, 35 prompts, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, 5 runs.
- Counting: one provider counts once per answer. Earlier internal figures (2,595 mentions) counted
  repeated mentions within one answer; this dataset counts 2,548.
- 10 spelling variants of provider names merged; names of individual persons removed.
- Sources: only rows belonging to the 525 valid measurements (discarded and replaced versions excluded).
- 35 measurements marked 'not recorded' for sources (limitation DP-1).
- "Own website as source in the same answer" (about 49 %): share of provider mentions, in answers with
  captured sources, where the normalised provider name appears in the domain stem of a source cited in
  the same answer (or vice versa, at least 4 characters). An approximation.
